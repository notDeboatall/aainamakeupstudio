FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Good to know before booking.

Do you travel for weddings across Odisha? +

Yes. Our senior artists and styling teams regularly travel to wedding venues and destinations across Odisha (such as Sambalpur, Rourkela, Berhampur, Balasore) and neighboring regions. Outstation travel and lodging arrangements are calculated transparently with your quote.

How early should I reserve my date? +

We recommend booking bridal dates 3 to 6 months in advance, especially during the auspicious wedding months (October through March). For party makeup and photoshoot projects, 2 to 4 weeks advance notice is typically sufficient.

Do you offer high-definition (HD) and airbrush options? +

Yes. We specialize in both precision HD brush application and silicone-based Airbrush artistry. We analyze your skin texture, venue lighting, and camera setups to recommend the technique that keeps your finish natural yet completely camera-ready.

What makeup products and brands do you carry? +

We work exclusively with internationally accredited professional cosmetic houses—including Charlotte Tilbury, MAC, NARS, Bobbi Brown, Huda Beauty, and Dior. All brushes and palettes are sanitized between every client according to strict clinical hygiene protocols.

Do you include a pre-wedding trial? +

Every bridal reservation includes an in-studio trial consultation. We evaluate your undertone, test foundation oxidization, draft eye contours, and review saree pinning with your bridal jewelry so there are zero surprises on your wedding day.

How are bookings confirmed and payments handled? +

A deposit confirms and blocks your slot exclusively on our master diary. The remaining balance is completed on the day of the appointment. We accept UPI, bank transfers, and cash at all studio branches.
